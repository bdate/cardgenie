Card Genie: personalized digital greeting cards with custom artwork

Card Genie creates a one-of-a-kind digital card for someone you love. You get two things made just for them: a custom image inspired by their hobbies, personality, and your relationship, and a personal message written for the occasion. No one else will have a card like it.

Tell Genie a few details or chat with the genie lamp, pick an art style like watercolor, storybook, or comic book, and add photos if you want the people you love drawn into the artwork. Send your digital card by email or text, or have it printed on cardstock and mailed.
