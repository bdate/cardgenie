Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Card Genie by Greeting Card Universe helps users create and deliver personalized digital greeting cards. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

We collect the card details a user provides, such as recipient name, relationship, occasion, message details, sender name, and generated card content. If a user chooses delivery, we collect the recipient email address or mobile phone number entered for that specific delivery.

How We Use Information

We use information to generate, display, revise, save, and deliver the requested card. Recipient email addresses and mobile phone numbers are used only to deliver the requested card notification and are not used for marketing or recurring messages.

SMS Privacy

Mobile phone numbers collected for Card Genie delivery are used only for one-time card delivery notifications. We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers or SMS consent with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Message Frequency and Rates

SMS message frequency is one message per card delivery request. Message and data rates may apply depending on the recipient's mobile carrier plan. Recipients can reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Service Providers

We may use service providers to host the app, generate card content, store card links, and deliver email or SMS notifications. These providers process information only as needed to support Card Genie.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us through Greeting Card Universe.
