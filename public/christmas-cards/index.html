What to write in a Christmas card

Share a little of your year and a warm wish for theirs. A personal line makes a holiday card stand out. A few starting points:

For family

Mention a tradition you share.

“Can’t wait for cocoa at the cabin. Merry Christmas — love you all.”

For friends

Recall a highlight from the year.

“From our camping disaster to your new house, what a year. Merry Christmas, friends!”

For a parent

Thank them for the memories.

“Every ornament has a story, and the best ones are about you. Merry Christmas, Mom.”

For clients

Keep it warm and grateful.

“Thank you for a wonderful year. Wishing you a joyful holiday season.”

For grandkids

Keep it magical.

“Santa told me you’ve been extra kind this year. Merry Christmas, little elves!”

Inclusive

Not sure what they celebrate? Go broad.

“Wishing you peace, joy, and warmth this holiday season and all year long.”

Christmas card questions

How much does a card cost?

Creating and previewing a card is free, and so is your first send. For current prices on sending and printed cards, see our FAQ.

Can I send our Christmas card to lots of people?

Yes. Make one card, then send it to as many email addresses or phone numbers as you like. Each person gets their own message with the card link.

Can the card look like our family?

Yes. Describe how they look, or add a clear close-up photo, and Genie draws them into the artwork in the art style you choose. Looks are used for the artwork only — they won’t show up in the inside message.

Can I change the message or the picture?

Yes. Edit the inside message yourself for free, switch between short, medium, and long versions, or ask Genie to redo the artwork.
