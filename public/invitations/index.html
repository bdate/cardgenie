What to write in an invitation

A good invitation answers who, what, when, where, and how to RSVP, with one warm line that makes guests want to come. A few starting points:

For a kids’ party

Lead with the theme and the age.

“Roar on over! Leo is turning 6 and wants you at his dinosaur party.”

For a shower

Name the guest of honor and the baby or couple.

“Join us for a garden brunch to shower Priya with love before her baby girl arrives.”

For a wedding or save the date

Keep it simple and let the date and place shine.

“Elena and Marco are getting married! Save the date, and a formal invitation will follow.”

For a surprise party

Remind guests to keep the secret and arrive early.

“Shh! We’re surprising Dad for his 60th. Please arrive by 6:30 so you’re hidden before he walks in.”

For a casual get-together

Say what to bring and keep the tone relaxed.

“Game night is back! Bring a snack and your most competitive spirit.”

For a celebration of life

Be gentle and say how guests can take part.

“Please join us to share stories and celebrate the life of Ruth, who loved this garden.”

Invitation questions

How much does a card cost?

Creating and previewing a card is free, and so is your first send. For current prices on sending and printed cards, see our FAQ.

What event details should I include?

Tell Genie the date and time, the place and address, and how guests should RSVP, plus anything else they need, like a dress code or what to bring. Genie puts these details inside the invitation under the message. If you leave out the date or place, Genie leaves a reminder to fill in, and Card Genie won’t send the invitation until you do.

Can I send it to my whole guest list?

Yes. Add up to 10 email addresses or cellphone numbers in the send panel at once, and send again for more guests. Each guest gets their own link to the invitation.

How do guests RSVP?

Guests reply the way you ask them to, by text, email, or phone. Just include it in your details, for example “RSVP to Sam by November 1.”

Can I mail printed invitations?

Yes. We print your invitation on premium cardstock and mail it by USPS.

Can I change the message or the picture?

Yes. Edit the inside message yourself for free, switch between short, medium, and long versions, or ask Genie to redo the artwork.
