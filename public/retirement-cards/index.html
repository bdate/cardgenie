What to write in a retirement card

Thank them for their years of work, share a memory, and cheer on what comes next. A few starting points:

For a coworker

Name what you’ll miss most.

“The office won’t be the same without your Monday stories. Enjoy every mile, Gary.”

For a boss

Thank them for what they built.

“Thank you for building a place we were proud to work. Fair winds and calm seas.”

For a parent

Celebrate the time they’ve earned.

“Forty years of hard work, Dad. Now go fix that swing — you’ve earned it.”

For a teacher

Acknowledge the lives they shaped.

“Thirty-five classes of kids carry a little of you with them. Thank you.”

Funny

Tease them about their new schedule.

“Congratulations on your new full-time job: doing whatever you want.”

From the whole team

Keep it warm and collective.

“From all of us: thank you for the years, the laughs, and the leadership. Happy retirement!”

Retirement card questions

How much does a card cost?

Creating and previewing a card is free. Sending it by email or text uses 3 credits, and new accounts get enough free credits for their first send. Credit packs start at $5 for 10 credits. A printed card mailed by USPS uses 10 credits.

Can I send it from our whole team?

Yes. Put the team name in the From field and send to one or more people. Each recipient gets their own message with the card link.

Can the card look like the retiree?

Yes. Describe how they look, or add a clear close-up photo, and Genie draws them on the cover in the art style you choose. Looks are used for the artwork only — they won’t show up in the inside message.

Can I change the message or the picture?

Yes. Edit the inside message yourself for free, switch between short, medium, and long versions, or ask Genie to redo the cover for 1 credit.
