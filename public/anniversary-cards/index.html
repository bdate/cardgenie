What to write in an anniversary card

Look back at one moment and forward to one plan. Shared details beat big declarations. A few starting points:

For a wife or husband

Recall the day you met or a small daily ritual.

“Ten years of Sunday mornings side by side, and you’re still my favorite chapter.”

For a partner

Celebrate the life you’ve built, quirks included.

“Five years, one three-legged dog, and a thousand miles of road. Let’s keep driving.”

For parents

Thank them for the example they set.

“Forty years of kitchen dances taught us what love looks like. Happy anniversary.”

For grandparents

Honor what their marriage gave the whole family.

“Fifty years ago you planted a tree. Look at the shade it gives us all now.”

For a first anniversary

Keep it light and look forward.

“One year down, and you’re still the only guy I’d fall for eleven times.”

For friends

Toast them as a couple.

“Here’s to the two people who make love look easy. Happy anniversary!”

Anniversary card questions

How much does a card cost?

Creating and previewing a card is free. Sending it by email or text uses 3 credits, and new accounts get enough free credits for their first send. Credit packs start at $5 for 10 credits. A printed card mailed by USPS uses 10 credits.

Can the card look like the two of us?

Yes. Describe how they look, or add a clear close-up photo, and Genie draws them on the cover in the art style you choose. Looks are used for the artwork only — they won’t show up in the inside message.

Can I add a photo of us together?

Yes. Add clear close-up photos of each person, and Genie uses them as a guide for the cover art in the style you pick. Group shots can inspire the scene, but faces match more closely from close-ups.

Can I change the message or the picture?

Yes. Edit the inside message yourself for free, switch between short, medium, and long versions, or ask Genie to redo the cover for 1 credit.
