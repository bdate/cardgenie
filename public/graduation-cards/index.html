What to write in a graduation card

Celebrate the work it took and the person they’ve become, then send them off with a wish. A few starting points:

For a high school grad

Name something they did that you admired.

“Four years of robots and late nights in the lab — the world is ready for what you build next.”

For a college grad

Honor what this degree means to the family.

“First in the family, and certainly not the last. We are so proud of you.”

For a grandchild

Share a hope for their future.

“Watching you grow has been my greatest joy. Go chase every dream.”

For a grad student

Recognize the long road.

“All those late nights paid off. Congratulations, counselor!”

For a little one

Keep it playful.

“Look out, first grade! A champion counter is on her way.”

With a gift

Tie it to their next step.

“A little something for your first apartment. Congratulations, graduate!”

Graduation card questions

How much does a card cost?

Creating and previewing a card is free. Sending it by email or text uses 3 credits, and new accounts get enough free credits for their first send. Credit packs start at $5 for 10 credits. A printed card mailed by USPS uses 10 credits.

Can the card look like the graduate?

Yes. Describe how they look, or add a clear close-up photo, and Genie draws them on the cover in the art style you choose. Looks are used for the artwork only — they won’t show up in the inside message.

Can I include their school colors or mascot?

Yes. Mention the colors, mascot, or campus details in Personal details, and Genie works them into the cover art.

Can I change the message or the picture?

Yes. Edit the inside message yourself for free, switch between short, medium, and long versions, or ask Genie to redo the cover for 1 credit.
