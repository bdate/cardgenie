What to write in a Father’s Day card

Dads love specifics. Name a skill he taught you or a moment you remember. A few starting points:

For Dad

Thank him for a skill he taught you.

“You taught me to measure twice and cut once. Turns out that works for life, too.”

Funny

Celebrate his signature move.

“Happy Father’s Day to the greatest air guitarist who never played a real chord. Rock on.”

For Grandpa

Honor the traditions he started.

“Every Saturday in the garage with you is a memory I’ll keep forever.”

For a new dad

Celebrate the moments that matter.

“Noah couldn’t ask for a better nap buddy. Happy first Father’s Day.”

For a stepdad

Thank him for showing up.

“You chose us, and you never stopped choosing us. Thank you.”

For a father figure

Recognize the role he played.

“You’ve been a dad to me in every way that counts. Happy Father’s Day.”

Father’s Day card questions

How much does a card cost?

Creating and previewing a card is free, and so is your first send. For current prices on sending and printed cards, see our FAQ.

Can the card look like my dad?

Yes. Describe how they look, or add a clear close-up photo, and Genie draws them into the artwork in the art style you choose. Looks are used for the artwork only — they won’t show up in the inside message.

Can it arrive on Father’s Day?

Yes. Email and text delivery is instant, so you can send it that morning. For a printed card, order early to allow time for USPS delivery.

Can I change the message or the picture?

Yes. Edit the inside message yourself for free, switch between short, medium, and long versions, or ask Genie to redo the artwork.
