What to write in a new baby card

Welcome the baby by name, celebrate the parents, and offer a wish for the little one. A few starting points:

For friends

Welcome the baby by name.

“Welcome to the world, Ivy! You picked the very best parents.”

For family

Celebrate the family growing.

“Our family just got a little bigger and a lot sweeter. We can’t wait to meet Theo.”

For coworkers

Keep it warm and practical.

“Congratulations on your new arrival! Wishing you joy and a few good nights’ sleep.”

For an adoption

Celebrate the homecoming.

“Welcome home, Grace. You are so loved already.”

For new grandparents

Celebrate their new title.

“Congratulations, Grandma and Grandpa! Hazel is lucky to have you.”

For a big sibling

Make them feel special too.

“Big sister June, you’re going to be the best teacher Theo could have.”

New baby card questions

How much does a card cost?

Creating and previewing a card is free, and so is your first send. For current prices on sending and printed cards, see our FAQ.

Can the card include the baby’s name?

Yes. Mention the baby’s name when you describe the card, and Genie can include it in the artwork and the message.

Can I send it as a printed card to the new parents?

Yes. Choose “Mail a printed card,” enter their address, and we’ll print it on cardstock and mail it by USPS.

Can I change the message or the picture?

Yes. Edit the inside message yourself for free, switch between short, medium, and long versions, or ask Genie to redo the artwork.
