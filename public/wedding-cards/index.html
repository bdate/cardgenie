What to write in a wedding card

Congratulate them as a couple, add a personal touch, and send them off with a wish for their life together. A few starting points:

For close friends

Recall when you knew they were meant to be.

“We knew it from the first dinner party. Here’s to a lifetime of sunsets together.”

For family

Welcome the new member.

“Kai, welcome to the family. You make our girl so happy.”

For a coworker

Keep it warm and simple.

“Wishing you both a lifetime of love and laughter. Congratulations!”

For a parent

Share your joy for them.

“Seeing you this happy is the best gift. Congratulations, Mom.”

With a gift

Tie it to their plans.

“A little something for the honeymoon fund. Enjoy every minute of Maui!”

If you can’t attend

Let them know you’re with them in spirit.

“So sorry to miss the big day. We’ll be raising a glass to you from here.”

Wedding card questions

How much does a card cost?

Creating and previewing a card is free. Sending it by email or text uses 3 credits, and new accounts get enough free credits for their first send. Credit packs start at $5 for 10 credits. A printed card mailed by USPS uses 10 credits.

Can the card look like the couple?

Yes. Describe how they look, or add a clear close-up photo, and Genie draws them on the cover in the art style you choose. Looks are used for the artwork only — they won’t show up in the inside message.

Can I send it on the wedding day?

Yes. Most cards are ready in a few minutes, and email or text delivery is instant. For a printed card, allow time for USPS delivery.

Can I change the message or the picture?

Yes. Edit the inside message yourself for free, switch between short, medium, and long versions, or ask Genie to redo the cover for 1 credit.
