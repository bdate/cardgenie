What to write in a Mother’s Day card

Thank her for something specific — the bigger the memory or the smaller the habit, the better. A few starting points:

For Mom

Thank her for a lesson you carry.

“You taught me to plant things and wait for them to bloom. Look at us now. Happy Mother’s Day.”

Funny

Celebrate her legendary something.

“Thanks for the love, the advice, and the lasagna. Mostly the lasagna.”

For Grandma

Honor her place in the family.

“Every stitch you taught us holds us together. Happy Mother’s Day, Grandma.”

For a new mom

Celebrate her first one.

“Watching you with Ellie is the most beautiful thing. Happy first Mother’s Day.”

For a stepmom

Thank her for choosing you.

“You didn’t have to show up for every game. You did anyway. Thank you.”

For a mother figure

Recognize the mothering, whoever it comes from.

“You’ve been a mom to me in all the ways that matter. Happy Mother’s Day.”

Mother’s Day card questions

How much does a card cost?

Creating and previewing a card is free. Sending it by email or text uses 3 credits, and new accounts get enough free credits for their first send. Credit packs start at $5 for 10 credits. A printed card mailed by USPS uses 10 credits.

Can the card look like my mom?

Yes. Describe how they look, or add a clear close-up photo, and Genie draws them on the cover in the art style you choose. Looks are used for the artwork only — they won’t show up in the inside message.

Can it arrive on Mother’s Day?

Yes. Email and text delivery is instant, so you can send it that morning. For a printed card, order early to allow time for USPS delivery.

Can I change the message or the picture?

Yes. Edit the inside message yourself for free, switch between short, medium, and long versions, or ask Genie to redo the cover for 1 credit.
