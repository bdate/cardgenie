SMS Opt-In for Card Genie

Last updated: August 5, 2026

Card Genie by Greeting Card Universe lets users create personalized digital greeting cards and choose whether to deliver a card by email or by one-time SMS/text message.

This page is provided so carrier reviewers can verify the Card Genie SMS call-to-action, consent language, and message flow.

How SMS Consent Works

A user starts at https://www.card-genie.com/, creates a digital greeting card, and then chooses a delivery method. If the user selects Cellphone delivery, they enter the recipient's cellphone number and must actively check an SMS consent checkbox before the card can be sent.

The SMS consent checkbox is unchecked by default. The Send button is disabled until the checkbox is checked. SMS consent is optional and is not required to create a card or use email delivery.

Consent Checkbox Language

Optional SMS delivery: I confirm this recipient agreed to receive a one-time SMS/text message from Card Genie with a link to this card. Message frequency is one message per card delivery request. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help. SMS consent is optional and is not required to create a card or use email delivery. See our Privacy Policy and Terms.

Message Type

Card Genie sends one-time delivery notifications only. We do not send marketing messages, recurring promotional messages, or unrelated alerts.

Example SMS Message

Your digital greeting card from [Sender Name] is ready: https://www.card-genie.com/?card=[card-id] Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help.
