SMS Opt-In for Card Genie

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Card Genie by Greeting Card Universe lets users create personalized digital greeting cards and choose whether to deliver a card by email or by one-time SMS/text message.

This page is provided so carrier reviewers can verify the Card Genie SMS call-to-action, consent language, and message flow.

How SMS Consent Works

A user starts at https://www.card-genie.com/, creates a digital greeting card, and then chooses a delivery method. If the user selects Text delivery, they enter the recipient's cellphone number and must actively check an SMS consent checkbox before the card can be sent.

The SMS consent checkbox is unchecked by default. The Send button is disabled until the checkbox is checked. SMS consent is optional and is not required to create a card or use email delivery.

Consent Checkbox Language

OK to text them this card — one message, no marketing. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. more   Privacy · Terms

Tapping "more" next to the checkbox shows: "Message frequency is one message per card delivery request. SMS consent is optional and is not required to create a card or use email delivery."

Program and Message Frequency

Program name: Card Genie card texts. Recipients get one text per card a sender chooses to deliver to them by text. Card Genie account holders may also get sign-in codes, thank-you notes from their recipients, and account notices they request. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. We do not send marketing messages, recurring promotional messages, or unrelated alerts.

Example Messages

Card delivery (the first and usually only message a recipient receives):

Card Genie: Rob, open your personalized card from Nasser. https://api.card-genie.com/c/[card-id] Msg & data rates may apply. Msg frequency varies. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

HELP response:

Card Genie: For help with card texts, email support@card-genie.com or visit card-genie.com/?contact=general. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.

STOP response:

Card Genie: You're unsubscribed and won't receive more texts from us. Reply START to resubscribe.

Help and Customer Care

Reply HELP to a Card Genie text for help, or email support@card-genie.com. You can also reach us through our contact form. Reply STOP at any time to opt out.
