What to write in a congratulations card

Name the achievement, recognize the effort behind it, and look ahead. A few starting points:

For a new job

Celebrate the fit, not just the title.

“Chicago just got a brilliant new architect. They’re lucky to have you.”

For a promotion

Acknowledge the years of work behind it.

“Ten years of showing up and leveling up. Nobody deserves this more.”

For a new home

Picture the memories to come.

“To many lazy evenings on that porch swing. Welcome home!”

For a big personal goal

Honor the training and the grit.

“26.2 miles and two years of early mornings. You are unstoppable.”

For an engagement

Celebrate them as a team.

“From summer camp to forever. We couldn’t be happier for you two.”

For a coworker

Keep it upbeat and professional.

“Congratulations on a well-earned win. It’s been great watching you lead.”

Congratulations card questions

How much does a card cost?

Creating and previewing a card is free. Sending it by email or text uses 3 credits, and new accounts get enough free credits for their first send. Credit packs start at $5 for 10 credits. A printed card mailed by USPS uses 10 credits.

Can the card look like them?

Yes. Describe how they look, or add a clear close-up photo, and Genie draws them on the cover in the art style you choose. Looks are used for the artwork only — they won’t show up in the inside message.

Can I send it from our whole team?

Yes. Put the team or group name in the From field, and send to one person or several. Each recipient gets their own message with the card link.

Can I change the message or the picture?

Yes. Edit the inside message yourself for free, switch between short, medium, and long versions, or ask Genie to redo the cover for 1 credit.
