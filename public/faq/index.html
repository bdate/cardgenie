Questions

Short answers for making and sending a Card Genie card.

Why create a Card Genie account?

Chat tools are great for freeform ideas. Card Genie is easier, more fun and powerful to use. Your account keeps a history of cards and sends, and you can email yourself a copy. Recipients get a clean card page—not a chat thread—and can thank you or make their own. Your number is only used to sign you in; recipients never see it. You can also have any card printed on cardstock and mailed by USPS.

You can design a card without registering. Confirming your mobile number saves your credits and sends, unlocks email and text delivery, and gives new accounts 2 extra credits.

How long does a card take?

Most cards are ready in a few minutes. Keep this page open while it generates. If it takes longer, come back to the same page — a finished card will appear without using credits twice.

What do credits cost, and when are they used?

Creating and previewing a card is free. You start with 2 free credits, and we add 2 more when you confirm your mobile number, which is enough for your first send.

  • Send a card to one person: 3 credits ($1.50 or less, depending on your pack)
  • Send the same card to 2 or more people: 2 credits per person (3 people = 6 credits)
  • Revise the cover, or have Genie rewrite the inside message: 1 credit
  • Edit the message yourself: free
  • Printed card, mailed by USPS: $5 (uses 10 credits)

Credits are used only when the send, revision, or print order succeeds. If something fails, your credits stay in your account. Credits never expire.

How much do credit packs cost?

  • 10 credits: $5
  • 21 credits: $10
  • 53 credits: $25
  • 108 credits: $50
  • 222 credits: $100 (best value, about 45¢ per credit)

Buy credits anytime from your account or when you send a card. Payment is handled securely by Stripe.

Why do I need a phone number to send?

Sending a card asks you to confirm your mobile number with a one-time text code. That creates your account so we can keep your credits and the cards you send, and new accounts get 2 extra credits when you confirm. You can still design a card before you register.

Will the recipient see my number?

No. Email and text deliveries come from Card Genie and include a link to the card. Your mobile number is used to sign you in, not shown on the card or to the recipient.

Can I send the same card again?

Yes. After a card is sent, use the send panel again for another email address or cellphone. Each recipient gets their own message with the same card link.

What if the email or text doesn’t arrive?

Ask the recipient to check spam or junk, then try sending again. For text, confirm the number and that they agreed to the one-time message. If it still fails, send us a note through our contact form.
