What to write in a thank you card

Be specific about what they did and how it made a difference. One or two sentences is plenty. A few starting points:

For a teacher

Name a change you saw in your child.

“Owen practiced his lines every night because you believed in him. Thank you.”

For a host

Recall one detail from the evening.

“Twenty chairs, three pies, and not one person left hungry — thank you for welcoming us in.”

For a gift

Say how you’ll use it or where it lives now.

“The quilt is already on our bed, and it feels like a hug every night. Thank you.”

For a coworker

Mention the project and the specific help.

“You stayed late to get the launch over the line. I couldn’t have done it without you.”

For a neighbor

Thank them for showing up without being asked.

“A fixed fence and a pot of chili — you showed up before we even asked. Thank you, Frank.”

For a caregiver or nurse

Acknowledge the care behind the job.

“Your patience and humor made a hard week so much easier. Our family is grateful.”

Thank you card questions

How much does a card cost?

Creating and previewing a card is free. Sending it by email or text uses 3 credits, and new accounts get enough free credits for their first send. Credit packs start at $5 for 10 credits. A printed card mailed by USPS is $5 (uses 10 credits).

Can I send the same thank-you card to several people?

Yes. After you send a card, use the send panel again for another email address or phone number. Each person gets their own message with the card link.

Can the card look like the person I’m thanking?

Yes. Describe how they look, or add a clear close-up photo, and Genie draws them on the cover in the art style you choose. Looks are used for the artwork only — they won’t show up in the inside message.

Can I change the message or the picture?

Yes. Edit the inside message yourself for free, switch between short, medium, and long versions, or ask Genie to redo the cover for 1 credit.
