What to write in a Valentine’s Day card

Specific beats sappy. Name something small they do that you love. A few starting points:

For a wife or husband

Celebrate an everyday ritual.

“Your Sunday pancakes may be shaped like potatoes, but they taste like love.”

For a new relationship

Keep it light and hopeful.

“Here’s to our first Valentine’s Day and to all the canyons we haven’t seen yet.”

For a long-distance partner

Count down to the reunion.

“Every mile between us is one I’d cross for you. See you in June.”

For friends

Celebrate your crew.

“Ten years of trivia nights and still undefeated. You’re my forever Valentines.”

For kids

Keep it sweet and simple.

“You’re my favorite little unicorn. Happy Valentine’s Day, Lily!”

Funny

Tease with love.

“I love you even when you steal all the blankets.”

Valentine’s Day card questions

How much does a card cost?

Creating and previewing a card is free, and so is your first send. For current prices on sending and printed cards, see our FAQ.

Can the card look like the two of us?

Yes. Describe how they look, or add a clear close-up photo, and Genie draws them into the artwork in the art style you choose. Looks are used for the artwork only — they won’t show up in the inside message.

Can it arrive exactly on Valentine’s Day?

Yes. Email and text delivery is instant, so you can send it the morning of. For a printed card, order early to allow time for USPS delivery.

Can I change the message or the picture?

Yes. Edit the inside message yourself for free, switch between short, medium, and long versions, or ask Genie to redo the artwork.
